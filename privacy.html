Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy applies to all apps published by TNL Software LLC ("we," "us," "our"). It explains what information our apps collect, how it's used, and your choices.

Current apps

Our currently published apps do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data at this time. They function entirely without a user account, network connection for personal data, or analytics.

Information our apps may collect

As we add features, a given app may collect some of the following. Each app's store listing will reflect what that specific app actually uses.

  • Usage & diagnostic data — general app usage statistics and crash reports, used to fix bugs and improve the app. Collected through standard platform tools (e.g. Google Play's built-in crash reporting).
  • Advertising identifiers — if an app shows ads, our ad provider (e.g. Google AdMob) may collect a device advertising ID and related data to serve and measure ads. See Google's ad policy for details. You can limit ad personalization through your device's ad settings.
  • Purchase status — if an app offers a paid "Pro" version or in-app purchase, the transaction is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We only receive confirmation that a purchase was made — we never see or store your payment card details.
  • User-provided data — if a future app includes accounts, saved content, or similar features, we'll collect only what's needed for that feature to work, and will update this policy and the app's data-safety listing accordingly before it ships.

Aggregated & anonymized data

We may analyze usage data in aggregate, de-identified form to understand general trends (e.g. how many people use a feature). We do not sell personal data that identifies an individual. Any data shared with third parties for analytics purposes is anonymized or aggregated first.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information our apps handle, consistent with the scale of a small independent developer. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

Your choices

You can request details about, or deletion of, any data associated with you by contacting us below.

Changes to this policy

We'll update this page as our apps' data practices change, and update the "Last updated" date above accordingly.
